How it worksThree messages, start to finish

Text it.
Slipp does the paperwork.

No forms or spreadsheets to learn. If you can send a message, you can keep proper books.

  1. 01

    Say what happened

    A sale, a debt, an expense. Write amounts the way you say them, like 45k, in English or Pidgin.

    Chidi took 2 rice and 1 palm oil. He paid by transfer
  2. 02

    The receipt prints itself

    Slipp works out the total from your saved prices, takes the items out of stock and makes a PDF receipt with your shop's details. One tap sends it to Chidi on WhatsApp.

  3. 03

    Ask about your money

    What you made this month, your best seller, who still owes you. The answer comes from your own records, in seconds.

    How much did I make this month?
