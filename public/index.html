02How it works

Text it. Slipp does the paperwork.

No forms or spreadsheets to learn. If you can send a message, you can keep proper books.

01

Say what happened

A sale, a debt, an expense. Amounts like 45k and casual English or Pidgin all work.

Chidi took 2 rice, he'll pay Friday
02

Get the receipt

Slipp makes the receipt or invoice, with your bank details, and a button to send it to your customer on WhatsApp.

Invoice INV-0042 for Chidi: ₦144,000
03

Ask anything

What you made this month, your best seller, who still owes you. The answers come from your own records.

Who still owes me?

03Who it's for

For the people behind the counter.

Fashion, food, phones, provisions. If you sell, you can text Slipp the way you'd text a friend.

04The product

The whole business, one screen away.

These are real screens from the demo shop. Open it and they're yours to try.

Overview

Profit you can trust, worked out as you sell.

Sales, spending, profit and money owed for the month, with a six-month chart and your best sellers.

Chat

Receipts come back in the chat.

Each sale gives you a PDF receipt and a button to send it to your customer.

Sales

Never forget a debt.

Unpaid invoices stay at the top, with a reminder ready to send and one tap to mark them paid.

Stock

Stock that counts itself.

Every sale takes items out of stock, and Slipp warns you before you run out.

05What it keeps for you

Everything a small shop needs to track.

Receipts and invoices

Clean PDFs with your name, address and bank details, sent on WhatsApp in a tap.

Payment reminders

A list of who owes you each morning, with the reminder message already written.

Stock and prices

Save a price once and just say "sold 3 rice". Low-stock warnings come by themselves.

Expenses

Fuel, rent, transport. Record them as they happen so your profit is honest.

Customers

Names and numbers saved from your sales, with what each one has paid and owes.

Browser or Telegram

Start on the web, then link Telegram. Both write to the same books.
